Loop Timer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Your privacy

Loop Timer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data from the app. You do not need an account. The app contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies.

Settings on your device

Your cue interval, repeat duration, preparation countdown, sound choice, appearance, and color theme are saved locally on your device. These preferences are used only to provide the timer and remember your choices. Loop Timer does not upload them to a developer-operated server or use its own cloud synchronization.

Timer and audio

Elapsed time is calculated on your device. Beep sounds are generated in the app, and spoken cues use Apple's system speech synthesis. Loop Timer does not record audio or access the microphone, contacts, location, photos, or Health data.

Retention and deletion

The app does not keep a history of your stretching sessions. Preferences remain until you change them or delete the app. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local settings. Device backups and restoring data are managed by Apple according to your device settings.

Apple services

Apple manages App Store downloads, device backups, and any platform diagnostics you choose to share. Those services are governed by Apple's privacy policies and your Apple settings. Loop Timer does not include its own diagnostic or analytics service.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact grtsharon@gmail.com. Information you voluntarily provide is used to respond to your request and is handled separately from the app's local timer data.

Changes

This policy describes this version of Loop Timer. If its data practices change, the policy and App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.
